Fire • Aerosols • Climate • Biogeochemistry

Douglas is an Earth System scientist working at the intersection of aerosols, climate, atmospheric chemistry, biogeochemistry, wildfire science, air quality, and environmental change. The lab combines models, observations, field data, and immersive visualization tools to understand how atmospheric processes shape climate, ecosystems, and society.

Elisa Bergas Massó

Postdoctoral Research Scholar
Aerosols • Dust • Ocean Biogeochemistry

My research explores how atmospheric aerosols shape the Earth System. By combining Earth System models and observations, I investigate how desert dust and biomass-burning aerosols influence ocean biogeochemistry, marine productivity, carbon sequestration, and ecosystem health.

Matthew Romm

PhD Student, Marine Science
Remote Sensing • Machine Learning • Phytoplankton

Matthew Romm is a marine science PhD student using satellite observations and machine learning to understand how aerosols and other environmental drivers influence nutrient availability and ocean phytoplankton activity. His research aims to improve our understanding of Earth System processes and marine biogeochemical cycles. Outside of research, Matthew enjoys hiking, exploring new places, and visiting museums.
