Join Us

Build the model. Question the model. Communicate what it means. We welcome students and collaborators interested in aerosols, climate, atmospheric chemistry, biogeochemistry, wildfire science, air quality, data visualization, and immersive learning.

Graduate Students

Prospective graduate students interested in modelling, observations, fire, aerosols, atmospheric chemistry, or ocean nutrient deposition are encouraged to reach out with a short statement of research interests.

Undergraduate Research

Undergraduate researchers can contribute to active projects involving coding, field data, instrumentation, visualization, science communication, and AR/VR development.

Collaborators

We welcome collaborations spanning atmospheric science, ecology, oceanography, public health, computer science, education, and environmental visualization.
